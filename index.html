      Instructions

        Geometry engine: manifold-3d (WASM) · loads on first run

        Drop an STL/OBJ/3MF or load the demo

        Your model becomes the master. Meltemi builds the printable mold box around it: conformal shells, parting board with key ridges, pins, clips, pour opening and vents — plus supports for the finished silicone form.

        —

          Drop STL / OBJ / 3MF / PLY to add masters

          No mold yet — load a master and press Generate.
          Out of date — regenerate

          How it works

          1. Master. Import the positive (STL/OBJ/3MF) or load the demo. Move/rotate it; the red parting plane splits the mold into halves A (pulled −Y) and B (pulled +Y).
          2. Mold box & parting board. Generate and print the conformal shells with flanges, ribs and pin holes, plus the parting board that carries the master-half reliefs and tongue-and-groove key ridges.
          3. Pour silicone. Sandwich the board between the shells, fit pins, clip the flanges and pour silicone into the open top — both halves at once.
          4. Remove the board. Demold; the two silicone halves now carry the cavity, pour channel, vents and the keys that align them.
          5. Supports + clips. Put the silicone halves back into the shells (or the lighter windowed cage) and clamp them with the casting clips.
          6. Pour wax / resin. Cast, let it cure, open the clips and demold. Repeat.

          Mode Support builds only the mother mold / cage for a silicone form you already have (import its outer surface).
